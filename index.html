One install. One server. Everyone on the LAN.

ZBZAI turns a single Windows computer into a private AI server. It finds its own network address and shows it, so anyone on the same Wi-Fi or LAN just opens that address.

Install in a minute

Run ZBZAI-Setup.exe, pick the drive and folder, done. Python is built in, no admin rights or account needed, and a clean uninstaller is added to Apps.

Open ZBZAI

Click the ZBZAI icon on the desktop or Start menu. ZBZAI becomes a server, opens in your browser and shows the LAN address to share and the admin PIN.

Everyone connects

Phones, tablets and PCs on the same Wi-Fi scan the QR code in ZBZAI's Share tab, or open http://your-ip:8765, and can add ZBZAI to their home screen as an app. Each person keeps their own conversations.

Ask about any file

Drop in up to ten files at once: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, OpenDocument, text or code. Ask across all of them, then get the edited file back in its own format.

Teach it, and it grows

Paste notes, upload documents, keep good answers. Every future answer is grounded in what ZBZAI has learned, with sources shown.

Coworkers do the work

Pick a Word writer, Excel analyst, PowerPoint builder or one you design, and export the result as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, Access, audio or video.

Updates and backs itself up

New version? One tap as admin, or run the newer setup: ZBZAI checks the fingerprint and restarts, keeping everything. Daily automatic backups, restore in a click.

Three free brains, chosen automatically

ZBZAI always uses the best brain available and falls back on its own if one stops working. All three are free: no keys, no subscriptions. Anyone who has their own key can add more.

Questions

Can ZBZAI run on my phone?

ZBZAI itself runs on one Windows PC (or a Linux or Mac computer): that computer is the server, with the AI model and everything ZBZAI learns. Phones and tablets do not run ZBZAI on their own; they use it over your local network. Connect them to the same Wi-Fi, scan the QR code in ZBZAI's Share tab, and add ZBZAI to the home screen if you like: it then opens like an app, with no internet. ZBZAI works on phones whenever the ZBZAI computer is on and ZBZAI is open.

Is ZBZAI really free? What about Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini?

Yes, ZBZAI is free, and so are all three of its brains: the local model, ZyeemZia AI online, and the knowledge vault. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Groq, Mistral, Grok and OpenRouter are optional extras for anyone who has their own key: paste it in Settings. Each service bills or limits its own key (Gemini and Groq have free tiers); nobody needs them.

Which files can ZBZAI read?

Up to ten at once: Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), PDF, OpenDocument, RTF, ebooks, CSV, text, Markdown, web pages and code. For pictures, sound and video it reads their details, and online the free ZyeemZia AI can also look at pictures. Ask across all the files, then save the edited version in its own format with one click.

Can ZBZAI make audio, videos or music?

Yes. Studio’s Video tool cuts, joins, captions, reshapes and converts videos and makes narrated videos from your photos and clips. Songs play as a real instrumental made from their chords and tempo, with a music video from your photos, and the words can be read over the music. Any answer becomes spoken audio (WAV or MP3), and the Video Explainer coworker makes narrated slide videos. On Windows; video and MP3 use the free ffmpeg tool. ZBZAI cannot sing yet.

Which files can ZBZAI make?

It can also write into your own Word, Excel or PowerPoint file, keeping its letterhead and design, and turn office files into PDF with the layout kept. Every answer and Studio document exports to Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), PDF, CSV, a SQLite database, a web page, Markdown, text and JSON, all offline. On Windows it also records audio (WAV) with the built-in voices and makes Access databases when Microsoft Office is installed. With the free ffmpeg tool it adds MP3 audio and narrated MP4 videos.

Can I make my own AI coworkers offline?

Yes. Open Coworkers, press New coworker, give it a name, a role and instructions, and pick its best export. Coworkers are shared on your network, use what ZBZAI knows, and work fully offline with a local model (run GET-LOCAL-MODEL.bat once). Online, they also work with the free ZyeemZia AI.

Is my copy genuine and safe?

Every official ZBZAI is sealed: each program file carries a fingerprint, checked every time ZBZAI starts. If anything has been changed, ZBZAI shows that the copy is not genuine and, when it is online, restores the official files by itself, keeping everything it has learned. Always download ZBZAI from zbzai.zyeemzia.com. ZBZAI is free to use; changing, unpacking or redistributing it is not allowed (see LICENSE.txt).

How do updates and backups work?

Whenever it is online, ZBZAI checks zbzai.zyeemzia.com for a new version. When one is ready, a banner appears; the admin presses Update now, ZBZAI downloads it, checks its fingerprint and restarts itself. Running a newer ZBZAI-Setup.exe does the same. Everything it has learned stays exactly as it was. ZBZAI also backs itself up every day (the last seven are kept), and Settings has Back up now, Restore and reset options, with a safety backup before every change.

Does ZBZAI really work with no internet?

Yes. With a local model installed (one download while online), ZBZAI runs entirely on your network. Without a model, it still answers from its knowledge vault, including its offline copy of the ZyeemZia AI knowledge library (switch it off in Settings if you prefer). The internet is only used for ZyeemZia AI or a brain you add with your own key, and you can turn both off in Settings.

How does it "become smarter day by day"?

Through its knowledge vault. Everything you teach (notes, documents, good answers) is stored and searched before every answer, so ZBZAI answers from your organisation's own knowledge, with sources. This is retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): fast, transparent and fully under your control. You can also move to a bigger local model at any time.

What computer do I need?

Any Windows 10 or 11 PC runs ZBZAI and the knowledge vault. For a local AI model, the small model runs on most PCs with 8 GB of memory; a graphics card with 8 GB or more makes medium and large models fast.

Is our data private?

Yes. The knowledge, conversations and settings stay in the data folder on the ZBZAI computer. With the local model or the vault, nothing leaves the network. When ZBZAI answers online (ZyeemZia AI, or a brain you add with your own key), the question and its matching passages are sent to produce the answer; nothing is stored there. Set the brain to Local only in Settings to keep every question inside the building.

Who can change settings?

Settings and removing knowledge need the admin PIN shown in the ZBZAI window. The admin can also limit teaching to the admin only.

Can others on the LAN not connect?

On the ZBZAI computer, set the Windows network to Private and allow Python through the firewall for private networks. Make sure both devices are on the same network, then open the address shown in the ZBZAI window.
